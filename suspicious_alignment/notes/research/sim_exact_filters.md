## Preliminary findings (to be replaced by the final text)

* E0: the two-phase curve and the monotone E[Alignment]-vs-m trend are reproduced; the late-time alignment
  agrees with Theorem 16 to within 0.001-0.006 (see `results/tables.md`, T0).
* E1, noise-dominated late phase (the regime of Deng's own experiment): every exact filter reproduces the
  SGD two-phase curve and the SGD late-time alignment (Theorem 16 evaluated at the filter's drift-equivalent
  step) to within 0.003; the filters act as a scalar rescaling of the noise matrix.
* E1, signal-dominated late phase (noise-free or large steps): the filters differ qualitatively from SGD
  (limit cycle in the top Hessian directions, alignment close to 1; SGD's alignment goes to 0).
* E2: at every trajectory state visited, for every filter, the expected alignment decreases below a critical
  step and increases above it (Deng's low-alignment dichotomy survives); the critical step is not controlled
  by 2/lam_1 for the filters in the signal-dominated regime (it scales with the gradient norm).
* E3: the window eta_D^loss < eta < eta_B^loss persists for all filters in the noise-dominated regime; in the
  signal-dominated regime the window is much narrower for Muon-type filters and the ordering REVERSES for
  large m (m=500: eta_D^loss / eta_B^loss = 2.3 for Muon at the isotropic initial state).
